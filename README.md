**Ember Thief** — a top-down stealth game. Play Pip, a small thief in a hooded cloak, sneaking through three moonlit gardens to steal every ember shard and slip through the moon gate unseen. Lantern Wardens patrol with vision cones; moonbloom bushes hide you, lantern light gives you away, and a thrown pebble can pull a guard off your trail. Sneak with Shift, listen for footsteps — a guard's steps grow louder as it nears — and never get caught.

- 3 hand-designed levels, each with its own patrols to learn
- Detection with a chance to recover: suspicion rises in sight, falls in hiding
- Noise system: walking is heard, sneaking is silent, pebbles distract
- Synthesised WebAudio sounds: footsteps, alarm stings, pickup chimes — no audio files

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
